## A Kubernetes Service is unable to forward traffic to its backend pods.

### Troubleshooting Steps:

```bash
# Step 1: Verify if endpoints exist for the service
kubectl get endpoints <service-name>

# Step 2: Ensure pod labels match service selector
kubectl get pods --show-labels

# Step 3: Debug internal connectivity
kubectl exec -it <pod-name> -- curl <service-name>:<port>
```

### Common Causes:
- Mismatch between pod labels and service selector
- Service selector not matching any pods

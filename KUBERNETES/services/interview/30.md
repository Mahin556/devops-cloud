#### Scenario 2: An application in a pod cannot connect to a database service named `postgres-db`.
**Detailed Troubleshooting Path:**
1.  **DNS Resolution:** Exec into the pod and try to resolve the service name.
    - Command: `kubectl exec -it <pod-name> -- nslookup postgres-db.default.svc.cluster.local`
    - **Expected:** Returns the `ClusterIP` of the service.
    - **If fails:** CoreDNS pods might be down (`kubectl get pods -n kube-system`).
2.  **Network Policy:** Check if a `NetworkPolicy` is blocking **Egress** from the app namespace or **Ingress** to the database namespace.
3.  **Service Selector:** Check `kubectl describe service postgres-db`. Does the `Selector` match the `Labels` on the actual database pod? *Common mistake: Typo in label `app: postgres` vs `app: postgres-db`.*
4.  **Port Mismatch:** Verify `targetPort` in Service definition matches `containerPort` in the DB Pod definition.

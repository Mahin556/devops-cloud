### 2. What is an Ingress(Native)?

- **Ingress Controller**: The actual software (like `nginx-ingress`, `traefik`, `aws-alb`) that runs in your cluster. It reads the Ingress rules and configures its internal proxy (e.g., `nginx.conf`) accordingly. It is the **entry point** for external traffic.
- **Ingress Resource**: The YAML manifest you define. It contains the **rules** (like "if host is `safiyan.com` and path is `/api`, send traffic to Service `api-service` on port 80").

**The Flow (From your note)**: `Internet` → `Ingress Controller` (Pod) → Reads `Ingress Resource` (Rules) → Forwards to `ClusterIP Service` → Forwards to `Pods`.

### 4. How the Ingress Flow Works in YOUR Cluster

Since your `ingress-nginx-controller` has an **External-IP: `<pending>`**, it means you are **NOT** in a cloud environment (like AWS/GCP). You are likely on a local VM, Minikube, or bare-metal.

**The Practical Flow for a User accessing `safiyan.com`:**

1.  **DNS Setup**: You configure `safiyan.com` to point to one of your Node's IPs (e.g., `192.168.1.10`).
2.  **User Request**: User hits `http://safiyan.com` on port 80.
3.  **NodePort Routing**: The request hits the Node's IP on port `80`. Your Ingress Controller Service is mapping port `80` to `NodePort 32722`. So the Node's iptables/kube-proxy routes traffic from port `80` to port `32722` on the node.
4.  **Ingress Controller Pod**: The traffic reaches the Ingress Controller Pod (Nginx).
5.  **Rule Matching**: The Ingress Controller looks at the `Host` header (`safiyan.com`). It matches a rule in your `Ingress` Resource.
6.  **Internal Routing**: The rule says "send `safiyan.com` to `nginx-service.default.svc.cluster.local:80`".
7.  **Service & Pod**: The traffic goes to the `ClusterIP` of `nginx-service` (`10.106.101.159`), which load-balances to your `nginx` Pods.

**The Golden Rule**: 
- **Ingress Resource** = The *Rulebook* (YAML).
- **Ingress Controller** = The *Doorman* (Pod) that reads the rulebook. 
- **Service** = The *Internal Address* (ClusterIP) the doorman sends you to.
- **`<pending>` External-IP** = You are on bare-metal/Local; you must access the Ingress Controller via its NodePorts (e.g., `192.168.1.10:32722`).

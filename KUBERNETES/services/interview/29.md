#### Q7: What do you know about Headless Service?
**Detailed Answer:**
A Headless Service is defined by setting `spec.clusterIP: None`.
**Normal Service:** Returns a **single** IP address (the ClusterIP) for load-balanced traffic.
**Headless Service:** Returns the **individual IP addresses of all Pods** in the selector set.

**Why is this needed?**
- **StatefulSets:** Databases like Cassandra, Elasticsearch, or Kafka need peer-to-peer discovery. They need to know the exact IP of *Pod A* vs *Pod B* to replicate shards or elect leaders.
- **Direct Communication:** If you want a client to connect to a specific replica bypassing the round-robin load balancing of `kube-proxy`.

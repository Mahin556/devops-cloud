### Why Odd Numbers are Always Used?

| Nodes | Quorum | Failures Tolerated | Split 50/50 Result |
|-------|--------|--------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | 1 | 0 | N/A |
| 2 | 2 | 0 | **Both sides fail** (1+1, quorum=2) |
| 3 | 2 | 1 | **One side wins** (2 vs 1) |
| 4 | 3 | 1 | **Both sides fail** (2+2, quorum=3) |
| 5 | 3 | 2 | **One side wins** (3 vs 2) |
| 6 | 4 | 2 | **Both sides fail** (3+3, quorum=4) |
| 7 | 4 | 3 | **One side wins** (4 vs 3) |

**Pattern:** Even numbers risk a 50/50 split where neither side has quorum. Odd numbers ensure one side always has majority.

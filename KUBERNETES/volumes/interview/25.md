## Persistent Volume Claim (PVC) fails to bind to a Persistent Volume (PV).

### Troubleshooting Steps:

```bash
# Step 1: Describe the PVC to check for errors
kubectl describe pvc <pvc-name>

# Step 2: Verify available PVs
kubectl get pv

# Step 3: Check if StorageClass matches
kubectl get storageclass
# or
kubectl get sc

# Step 4: Update PVC to use correct StorageClass
# In PVC YAML:
storageClassName: <correct-storage-class>
```

### Common Causes:
- Incorrect StorageClassName in PVC
- No matching PV available
- StorageClass provisioner not configured

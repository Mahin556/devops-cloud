## What is Ingress and Ingress Controller?

**Definition:** Ingress Controller is a **software-defined Application Load Balancer (Layer 7)** that runs **inside the Kubernetes cluster**.

### Why Ingress Was Introduced:
- To avoid provisioning multiple external load balancers
- To bring load balancing into the **Kubernetes admin's scope**
- To reduce dependency on external LB/network teams

### Benefits Over External Load Balancer:
1. **Single LB for multiple services** - One Ingress can route to many services
2. **Cost reduction** - No need to provision multiple cloud LBs
3. **Operational simplicity** - No need to reach out to LB/firewall teams repeatedly
4. **Path-based routing** - Route based on URL paths
5. **Host-based routing** - Route based on domain names

### Popular Ingress Controllers:
- **NGINX Ingress Controller** (most popular open-source)
- **HAProxy Ingress Controller** (default in OpenShift/Red Hat)
- Traefik, Istio, etc.

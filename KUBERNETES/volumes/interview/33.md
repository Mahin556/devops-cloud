## What is Reclaim Policy in PV? How Many Types? What is `persistentVolumeReclaimPolicy` in a PV manifest? What does it manage?

**Answer:** The reclaim policy defines what happens to the PV and its data when the corresponding **PVC is deleted**.

### Three Types of Reclaim Policy:

**1. Recycle:**
- When PVC is deleted, PV goes back to **Available** state
- The DATA on the volume is **scrubbed/erased** (cleaned up)
- PV can be claimed by another PVC
- **Important:** This is deprecated and not recommended for production

**2. Retain:**
- When PVC is deleted, PV goes to **Released** state
- The DATA is **preserved** on the volume
- PV is NOT available for a new claim immediately
- Administrator must manually review and clean up
- Use case: Financial data, compliance, critical data that cannot be auto-deleted

**3. Delete:**
- When PVC is deleted, BOTH PV and the underlying storage data are **deleted**
- Everything is removed automatically
- Use case: Temporary storage, development environments

### Summary Table:

| Policy | PVC Deleted | PV State After | Data | PV Reusable? |
|--------|-------------|----------------|------|--------------|
| **Recycle** | PV goes to Available | Available | Scrubbed (erased) | Yes, by anyone |
| **Retain** | PV goes to Released | Released | Preserved | Only after manual admin cleanup |
| **Delete** | PV deleted | Deleted | Deleted | No |

### Key Point from Transcript:
"Recycle means when you lose PVC, PV goes back to Available state. Retain means PV goes to Released state - no one can claim it until admin manually makes it Available again."

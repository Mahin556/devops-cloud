## I have a 100GB PV. Can I create two PVCs of 50GB each from the same PV?

**Answer:** **NO**

### Explanation:
- PV cannot be logically broken/split into smaller pieces
- A 100GB PV will be given entirely to one PVC
- If your PVC requests 50GB and only 100GB PV is available:
  - The 100GB PV WILL be allocated to you
  - You get the entire 100GB, not just 50GB
- You cannot hold 50GB and leave 50GB for another PVC

### What Happens:
- If all available PVs are 100GB and your claim is 50GB
- You get a 100GB PV (the condition matches: size ≥ 50GB)
- The remaining 50GB is wasted (cannot be used by another PVC)

### Solution for Efficient Storage:
- Use **Dynamic Provisioning** instead of Static PVs
- Dynamic provisioning creates exactly the size you request
- No waste, no shortage
